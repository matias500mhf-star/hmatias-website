Delete your KARTA online account

KARTA Identity Wallet is a product of HMATIAS – Prestação de Serviços SU, LDA. If you no longer have access to the app, you can use this page to request deletion of your online KARTA account and associated service data.

Inside the app

When the online service is active, open Settings → Account and online backup → Delete online account. Confirmation requires the account password.

Without access to the app

Email geral@comercialhmatiasps.com with the subject KARTA — Account deletion request and provide only the email address used for the account.

Request deletion by email

What is deleted

After legitimate verification of the request, the online account, authentication sessions, encrypted online backup and account-dependent service records are deleted. HMATIAS does not ask for the wallet PIN, backup password or copies of identity documents to process the request.

Local data on the device

Deleting the online account does not automatically erase the local wallet. To remove local wallet data, use Delete wallet from this device in KARTA Settings or uninstall the app.
