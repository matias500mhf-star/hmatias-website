CONSTRUCTION IN LUANDA

Practical support for works, remodelling and business interventions.

HMATIAS provides civil construction services in Luanda by structuring each assignment around the client's actual requirement. The process begins with an initial assessment, followed by scope definition, identification of materials and resources, timing and the agreed execution plan.

For companies and institutions, work may range from concrete, paving and supporting infrastructure to office remodelling, sanitary facilities, roofing, finishes and corrective building interventions. The objective is to keep execution organised, communication direct and commercial conditions defined before work starts.

When a project also requires ongoing maintenance or procurement of materials and equipment, requirements can be coordinated with Facilities & Maintenance and Supply & Procurement, while keeping each responsibility clearly defined.
