FACILITIES IN LUANDA

Operational continuity depends on organised maintenance.

A business facility needs predictable responses for maintenance, cleaning, upkeep and smaller operational requirements. HMATIAS structures the service around the characteristics of the location, required frequency and clearly assigned responsibilities.

For recurring services, the objective is to reduce improvisation and concentrate activities in a clear service plan. For one-off requirements, each intervention is defined case by case, with scope, resources, timing and commercial conditions confirmed in advance. Preventive maintenance can be scheduled where assets, areas or routines justify periodic support; corrective maintenance is assessed according to the nature of the occurrence and the available intervention capability.

Where a requirement involves building works, remodelling or specific procurement, it can be coordinated with Construction & Infrastructure and Supply & Procurement, while keeping commercial responsibilities clearly separated.
