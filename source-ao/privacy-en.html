PRIVACY

Necessary data. Minimal exposure.

Source AO is designed to search, verify and coordinate sourcing without turning private contacts, messages or documents into public information.

Launch version · 25 September 2026. This notice describes the intended operation of Source AO v1. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

1. Data processed

  • search terms, category, specification, quantity, priority and location;
  • name, company or institution, customer type and contact details voluntarily supplied with a sourcing or quotation request;
  • business details, tax or registration number, capabilities, location, references and professional contact details submitted in a partner-network application;
  • products, quantities, specifications, destination, deadline, purchasing intent, accepted alternatives, indicative budget and commercial RFQ notes;
  • structured supplier responses to confirmation requests;
  • status, dates and operational history of requests and verifications;
  • minimum technical metadata for security, auditing and abuse prevention.

2. Purposes

We process this data to handle sourcing requests, locate potential suppliers, verify commercial information, track requests, assess supplier/subcontractor/partner applications, improve search quality, produce aggregated demand analysis and protect the platform against abuse.

3. Requester contact details

Contact details submitted with a request are encrypted in the backend and are not shown in public search or public request tracking. Access is limited to authorised HMATIAS operations.

The profile and full RFQ details are encrypted and accessible to authorised HMATIAS operations. The private tracking link displays the products and status without returning the customer’s name, company, contact details or budget.

When a request is marked completed or closed, direct contact details and the full RFQ profile are automatically deleted after 180 days. The operational request summary may remain for history and aggregated demand analysis.

4. Security and abuse prevention

Source AO applies server-side validation, route-level rate limits, hashed private tokens, contact encryption and separation between public, internal and restricted data.

For abuse prevention, the network address is not stored in plain text: it is transformed into a pseudonymised HMAC identifier, and the related rate-limit windows are deleted after 48 hours.

5. Technical logs

Application logs record only operational information such as request identifier, method, route, status and duration. They do not record search text, contact details, request bodies, confirmation tokens or private tracking tokens.

Infrastructure logs follow the short retention period provided by the infrastructure provider for the active account.

6. Suppliers, sources and evidence

Source AO may retain references to public sources and verification records for auditing and reconfirmation. A public reference or catalogue is not treated as proof of current stock.

Source AO v1 does not accept uploads of screenshots, PDFs, photographs, email files, chat exports or other private attachments. Commercial documents that need to be retained are handled outside the public Source AO API, in authorised business systems.

7. Freshness and reconfirmation

Commercial confirmations are associated with a date/time and a validity window. Once that window expires, information is marked for reconfirmation rather than continuing to be presented as current.

8. Sharing and publication

  • the customer’s private contact details are not published;
  • private supplier messages are not published as search results;
  • only reviewed, minimum commercial provenance may appear publicly, such as supplier, verification method, date and validity;
  • restricted data is not placed in the product’s public repository.

8-A. Internal request and partner notifications

Submitted sourcing requests and partner applications may be stored in a private notification queue without publishing personal data. When the business delivery channel is configured, HMATIAS may email its commercial team the request reference, necessary requirements and supplied contact details for follow-up. Any technical email provider used processes the data strictly to deliver that communication. Registering a request does not mean the notification was delivered or read.

Do not include bank details, passwords or identity documents in free-text fields. For questions about data processing, use the official contact published on the HMATIAS website.

9. Cookies, consent and analytics

Source AO uses the Cookiebot consent mechanism on the HMATIAS domain. Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4 are loaded by the HMATIAS implementation only after analytics consent. Measurement may record general commercial interactions such as search use, opening an RFQ and technical confirmation that a request was registered.

Source AO analytics events do not send Google search text, product descriptions, name, company, phone, email, budget, request notes, private reference or tracking token. Non-essential technologies remain subject to the user’s consent choices.

10. Data-related requests

Requests to access, correct, restrict or delete data should be sent to HMATIAS through the official channels published on the corporate website. Each request will be assessed according to the record type, its purpose and applicable obligations.
