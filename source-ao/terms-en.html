TERMS OF USE

Useful research, without false guarantees.

Source AO organises commercial research, supplier discovery, verification and sourcing requests. The platform distinguishes information found from current commercial confirmation.

Launch version · 25 September 2026. These terms apply to Source AO v1 and may be updated as the service evolves.

1. Purpose

Source AO helps users structure purchasing needs, locate materials, equipment, services and potential suppliers, assess public evidence and request sourcing support from HMATIAS.

2. Information states

Potential supplier means a relevant match exists, not that a product is available. Source checked means identifiable public evidence exists. Commercial confirmation requires current, traceable confirmation for the specific requirement.

When a confirmation expires, its state must change to reconfirmation; it must not silently be treated as current availability.

3. Price, stock, quantity and lead time

Prices, quantities, stock, delivery lead times and commercial terms may change. Information checked at a particular time is not a permanent guarantee. A purchase becomes binding only under the terms of an applicable proposal, quotation, order, contract or other commercial acceptance.

4. Sourcing requests

By registering a request, the user authorises HMATIAS to process the supplied data to research the market, contact potential suppliers when necessary, organise responses and track the process. Submitting a request does not automatically create an obligation to buy, sell or supply.

5. Public sources and suppliers

Catalogues, websites, directories and other public sources may be incomplete, outdated or contain errors. Source AO aims to show confidence and verification date, but commercial decisions should rely on the most recent confirmation available.

A company’s presence on the platform does not imply representation, exclusivity, partnership, stock guarantee or commercial approval by HMATIAS.

6. Human review

Supplier responses intended to raise verification status are reviewed before being promoted to public information. The system must not automatically turn a private response into a public claim about stock or price.

7. Responsible use

Users must not bypass security controls, automate abusive requests, manipulate confirmations, enter false data, attempt to obtain third-party private data or use the platform for improper contact harvesting.

8. Service availability

Search, Radar, tracking or confirmations may be temporarily limited or suspended for maintenance, security, data correction or source unavailability. When critical information cannot be validated, Source AO must fail conservatively.

9. Relationship with HMATIAS

Source AO is a service of HMATIAS. Purchasing, brokerage, supply, logistics, execution or procurement services may be subject to a separate proposal, margin, commission, payment terms or contract.

10. Privacy

Data processing on Source AO is described in the Privacy Notice. Source AO v1 does not accept private attachments through the public API or supplier-confirmation pages.
