Privacy Policy

HMATIAS – Prestação de Serviços SU, LDA seeks to handle responsibly the information received through its website and contact channels.

1. Information you may provide

When contacting HMATIAS you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, company, e-mail, phone/WhatsApp number and a description of the product, service, work or supply requirement.

2. Purpose

This information is used to respond to requests, prepare quotations, clarify availability, organise commercial contact and follow services or supplies requested.

3. Form and WhatsApp

The main website form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp so the visitor can choose whether to send it. Final sending occurs through WhatsApp and is subject to that platform's terms and privacy policy.

4. Virtual assistant

The website provides a virtual assistant for initial guidance on services, products and quotation requests. Messages sent to the assistant may be processed through technological infrastructure used by HMATIAS to generate a response. Do not send passwords, banking data, identity documents or other sensitive information through the assistant.

5. External links

The website may include links to external services such as WhatsApp and LinkedIn. When opening those pages, the respective providers' terms and policies apply.

6. Retention and access

HMATIAS retains commercial information only for the period necessary to respond to the request, manage the commercial relationship or comply with applicable obligations. Access should be limited to people who need the information to provide the service or response.

7. Privacy contact

For questions related to data provided to HMATIAS, contact geral@hmatiasps.ao.
