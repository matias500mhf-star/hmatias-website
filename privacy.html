Privacy Policy

HMATIAS – Prestação de Serviços SU, LDA seeks to handle responsibly the information received through its website and contact channels.

1. Information you may provide

When contacting HMATIAS you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, company, e-mail, phone/WhatsApp number, operational or delivery location, and information needed to describe a service, product, work, supply or appointment request.

2. Purpose

This information is used to respond to requests, structure quotations, assess availability, prepare commercial contact, organise appointment requests and follow services or supplies requested.

3. Forms, quotation requests and WhatsApp

Website forms may organise the information you provide and prepare a structured message for the HMATIAS commercial WhatsApp. You review the message and decide whether to send it. Final sending occurs through WhatsApp and is subject to that platform's terms and privacy policy.

HMATIAS Clean and HMATIAS Supply requests may include lists of products, materials, technical references, quantities, location and commercial notes. The HMATIAS Clean quotation cart uses temporary browser session storage to retain selected items during the visit; it is not used as a payment system or customer account.

4. Appointment requests

The appointment form collects only information needed to request a preferred meeting time. Submitting the request does not automatically confirm a date, time, contract or payment.

5. Virtual assistant

The website provides a virtual assistant for initial guidance on services, products and quotation requests. Messages sent to the assistant may be processed through technological infrastructure used by HMATIAS to generate a response. Do not send passwords, banking information, identity documents or other sensitive information through the assistant.

6. External links

The website may include links to external services such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram, manufacturer catalogues and other relevant pages. When opening those services, the respective providers' terms and policies apply.

7. Retention and access

HMATIAS retains commercial information only for the period necessary to respond to the request, manage the commercial relationship or comply with applicable obligations. Access should be limited to people who need the information to provide the service or response.

8. Usage statistics

The website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand pages viewed, visit sources, device characteristics and commercial interaction patterns. The HMATIAS integration may measure service-area views, service navigation, clicks on telephone, e-mail or WhatsApp contact channels, quotation calls-to-action, project engagement, valid form submissions and handoff attempts from a website form to WhatsApp.

These analytics events use technical or commercial categories such as contact method, service area, form type and internal project identifier. HMATIAS does not send names, telephone numbers, e-mail addresses, form contents, prepared WhatsApp message text, quotation contents or virtual-assistant conversations to Google Analytics as custom event parameters.

A form handoff to WhatsApp is measured as a handoff attempt and is not treated as a confirmed commercial lead. The integration does not enable Google Signals or advertising personalisation signals. You can manage or block cookies in your browser settings and read how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

9. Privacy contact

For questions related to data provided to HMATIAS, contact geral@hmatiasps.ao.
