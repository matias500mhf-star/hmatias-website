SUPPLY REQUESTS

Before requesting a quotation.

01

Can I request supply to other provinces?

We accept enquiries from across Angola and from international buyers seeking supply into Angola. HMATIAS holds no own stock: supplier availability, delivery location, transport and lead time are assessed for each request before a proposal is submitted.

02

What information is needed for a quotation?

Provide the reference or technical description, standard and dimensions where applicable, quantity and unit of measure, delivery location and target date. Photographs, technical data sheets and catalogue item codes help identify the product. You can register a quotation request with these details.

03

How are transport, lead time and certificates confirmed?

Specify your delivery requirements and required technical documents in the request. We consult the supplier to confirm availability, technical data sheets and applicable certificates. Transport costs, lead time and supply documentation are defined in the proposal before order award.

Looking for a specific material, product or equipment?

Provide the reference, standard, dimensions, quantity, delivery location and target date. We consult suppliers and prepare a proposal; HMATIAS does not advertise owned inventory.
