WHY WORK WITH HMATIAS?

One request, a coordinated process.

Our role is not to replace manufacturers or claim unlimited availability. We reduce the work of supplier research, comparison and fulfilment coordination for the customer.

01

One point of contact

We organise requirements across different materials, consult suitable suppliers and consolidate responses for evaluation.

02

Technical and commercial comparison

We check references, permitted alternatives, documents, costs, lead times and terms before presenting a supply proposal.

03

Order-to-delivery follow-up

After award, we coordinate purchasing and logistics under the agreed terms and follow the order through to delivery.
