NEXT DESIGN STAGE

Technical validation before construction

The current package improves the site-layout presentation. A complete execution set requires the following surveys and discipline-specific designs:

01

Topographic survey and confirmation of plot boundaries, easements and statutory setbacks.

02

Architectural design: room dimensions, openings, heights, roof slope, ventilation and accessibility.

03

Structural and civil engineering: foundations, steel profiles, connections, bracing, floor loads and drainage.

04

Fire-safety, electrical, water and sanitation installations, plus vehicle turning and access checks.

These drawings are preliminary site-layout studies based on the dimensions and area schedules currently available. They are not licensed architectural or engineering drawings and must not be used for construction, fabrication, permitting or procurement without professional validation.
