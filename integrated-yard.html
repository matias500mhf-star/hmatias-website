OPERATIONAL PURPOSE

Planned functions of the facility

The proposed yard will serve as an operational base for HMATIAS commercial, logistics and construction activities.

01

Material storage

Dedicated areas for receiving, storing and dispatching materials, organised by product category.

02

Distribution and operations

Access routes and handling areas planned to support the daily movement and distribution of materials.

03

Administrative support

Space for customer service, recording incoming and outgoing goods, and coordinating daily operations.

Current project status. The project remains at preliminary site-layout stage. These dimensioned concept studies do not replace the architectural and engineering designs, statutory approvals or construction documents required before work begins.

Before construction, the selected option must be developed using a verified topographic survey, confirmed plot boundaries and statutory setbacks, together with architectural, structural, drainage, fire-safety and building-services designs.
