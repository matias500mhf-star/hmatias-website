OPERATIONAL PURPOSE

What the facility is intended to support

The project is planned as an operational base, not as a completed public works case study.

01

Material storage

Protected stock areas and a layout that supports goods reception and dispatch.

02

Distribution and operations

Yard circulation and handling areas designed around the daily movement of materials.

03

Administrative support

A customer-service office and support space for sales, records and operational coordination.

Current project stage. The project remains at preliminary site-layout stage. The plans are dimensioned concept studies for communication; they are not execution drawings, an approved permit set or authorisation to build.

Before construction, the selected option requires confirmation of the plot survey and boundaries, statutory setbacks, architectural design, structural calculations, floor and drainage design, access geometry, fire safety and building services.
