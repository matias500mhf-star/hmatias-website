PRODUCT STATUS

A secure foundation before advanced capabilities.

KARTA remains in development. This page presents only capabilities and objectives supported by the current project state; it does not claim store availability, certification or future features as already completed.

Current foundation

  • Account registration and authentication
  • Secure wallet access
  • Identity profile
  • Architecture for document metadata and encrypted document storage
  • Audit trail
  • Mobile-first experience

Positioning

KARTA is presented as a HMATIAS digital product in development. The goal is a clear digital identity-wallet experience while keeping the technology product separate from HMATIAS operational construction, facilities, supply and business-service activities.

Commercial status: pre-launch / Alpha. It is not currently presented as a publicly downloadable application.
