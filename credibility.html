HOW WE MANAGE TRUST

What HMATIAS states — and what we do not present as fact without confirmation.

Professional proposals do not require vague promises. We prefer defined scope, commercial conditions and responsibilities before creating expectations.

✓
Real project portfolioPortfolio content uses real HMATIAS work. Client, location and dates are disclosed only when appropriate or authorised.
✓
Confirmed prices and lead timesPrice, stock, logistics and delivery timing remain subject to confirmation when not explicitly published.
✓
No invented partnershipsReferences to manufacturers, suppliers or third parties are not presented as exclusivity or institutional partnership without an express basis.
✓
Scope before executionConstruction, facilities and supply work is structured from the client's requirement, specification and operating conditions.
✓
Privacy by designThe website assistant should not request passports, identity cards, passwords or banking details to answer general questions.
