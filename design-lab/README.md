# HMATIAS — Design Lab 2026

This directory is an isolated, non-production visual prototype. It does not replace or modify the approved homepage or any production page.

## Design direction
- Preserve the approved HMATIAS institutional blue identity.
- Use a restrained editorial layout, stronger typographic hierarchy and generous spacing.
- Make navigation buyer-oriented and consistent across desktop and mobile.
- Use only existing, verifiable HMATIAS assets; do not invent project photography or claims.
- Give each service and resource a distinct purpose and destination.
- Keep motion subtle, purposeful, accessible and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Prioritize mobile performance, accessibility, clear calls to action and SEO structure.

## Content direction
- Remove repeated institutional language, generic promises and duplicated explanations.
- Keep each page focused on what HMATIAS does, concrete service scope, verifiable work and one clear contact action.
- Prefer short descriptions and real evidence over long methodology or FAQ blocks.
- Keep forms only where they collect information needed to prepare a real quotation or service request.

## Review scope
1. Header and navigation behavior.
2. Hero composition and visual hierarchy.
3. Service discovery and page entry points.
4. Project evidence and editorial content distinction.
5. Contact conversion and responsive behavior.

## Safety
The prototype is not linked from the live website and must not be deployed to production without review and explicit approval.
